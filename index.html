About Us

Trusted electrical engineering partner for industries across Madurai and Tamil Nadu

JR Engineering is a leading EA Grade licensed electrical contracting firm based in Madurai. We specialize in the supply, installation, testing, and maintenance of high and medium voltage electrical systems for industrial, commercial, and institutional clients.

With decades of hands-on experience, our team delivers reliable, efficient, and safety-compliant electrical solutions — from new installations to critical breakdown repairs. We handle TANGEDCO approvals, electrical design & drawings, and full project execution under one roof.

Our Services

Comprehensive electrical engineering solutions for every need

HV/MV Equipment

Supply and installation of transformers, generators, switchgear, and circuit breakers for high and medium voltage systems.

Motors & Cable Systems

Installation of industrial motors, capacitors, HT/LT cables, control cables, and complete cable management systems.

Panels & Distribution

Electrical panel boards, distribution boxes, light fittings, earthing systems, and internal wiring for all building types.

Design & Approvals

Professional electrical design, drawing preparation, load calculations, and TANGEDCO liaison for regulatory approvals.

Testing & Inspection

Comprehensive inspection, testing, and commissioning of electrical systems including protection relay calibration.

Emergency Repairs

24/7 emergency breakdown services for transformers, circuit breakers, and critical electrical infrastructure.
